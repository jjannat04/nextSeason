## Brand & Style

This design system translates Earth-observation remote sensing into actionable, micro-level agronomic foresight. Tailored for researchers, policymakers, agricultural extension workers, and regional farming co-operatives in South Asia and global deltaic basins, the interface balances astronomical precision with grounded terrestrial utility.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Scientific Minimalist with Planetary Precision**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Calm, authoritative, analytical, and luminous. It evokes the feeling of peering through an orbital dashboard down onto living hydrological and vegetative systems.
- **Visual Stance:** Completely eschews neon-drenched sci-fi clichés, generic corporate fintech gradients, and naive folk-art rustic styles. The interface feels like an official aerospace environmental observatory—rational, disciplined, and profoundly reliable.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Conceived from inception to host dual typography (English and Bangla) with identical vertical balance, baseline alignment, and typographic hierarchy, ensuring equitable access from Dhaka agritech hubs to rural Union Parishad advisory desks.

## Colors

The palette establishes an orbital-to-terrestrial color journey. High-depth space navies anchor analytical authority, while high-clarity satellite cyans, crop-chlorophyll emeralds, and alluvial soil ochres articulate physical data domains.

### Color Roles & Meaning
- **Primary (`#0A1128` Deep Space Navy):** Represents orbital depth and firm structural grounding. Used for primary branding elements, primary display typography, top-level navigation masts, and primary actions. Secondary dark navy tone (`#0E1A38`) drives high-density telemetry dashboards.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` NASA Satellite Blue):** Represents orbital sensors, precipitation radars (GPM IMERG), surface moisture telemetry, and atmospheric datasets. Accompanied by `#0369A1` for active links/states and `#38BDF8` for atmospheric data vectors.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D` Vegetative Emerald):** Encodes photosynthetic vitality, NDVI/EVI health indices, optimal crop windows, and positive yield forecasts. Complemented by `#22C55E` for live status indicators.
- **Earth Accent (`#D97706` Alluvial Ochre / Amber):** Sourced from fertile topsoil, silt sedimentation, and harvest maturity indices. Accompanied by deep amber (`#B45309`) for drought stress indicators and climate hazard warnings.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` Pure Off-White):** A crisp, low-glare surface that reduces eye fatigue under prolonged field and terminal usage. Paired with structural slate dividers (`#E2E8F0` / dark mode `#1E293B`).

## Typography

The typographic system provides clinical legibility across high-density remote-sensing dashboards, satellite raster overlays, and agronomic field guides.

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet friendly, with modern optical balance that holds weight and authority across large telemetry titles and analytical metric cards.
- **Body & Data Telemetry (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral sans-serif designed for micro-screens, dense tables, and scientific figures. Inter's tall x-height and tabular number alternates guarantee vertical stability when parsing continuous SMAP soil-moisture percentages or NASA POWER solar irradiance figures.

### Bilingual Handling (English / Bangla)
- When rendering Bangla scripts, font rendering relies on system-level Unicode fallbacks with matching cap heights.
- All vertical line heights maintain a minimum 1.45× ratio to avoid glyph clipping of matras and conjuncts (juktakkhor) in agricultural advisory cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured **Instrument Grid System**:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column dynamic grid with a `1.5rem` (24px) gutter and a maximum container width of `1440px`. Accommodates side-by-side geospatial mapping canvases and analytical telemetry sidebars.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters; secondary sensor panels collapse into swipeable cards or slide-over drawer trays.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive layout with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe-area canvas margins. Maps occupy pinned full-width viewports with collapsible bottom sheets for localized crop advisories.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Built strictly on an 8px base grid (4px sub-grid for badges, chips, and micro-metrics). Spacers provide visual breathing room between raw data points, preventing telemetry fatigue during long analysis sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Crisp Low-Contrast Outlines**, avoiding heavy dramatic drop-shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Canvas (Base Level 0):** Pure `#F8FAFC`. Houses background spatial coordinates, low-contrast terrain layers, and passive dashboard frames.
2. **Surface Layer 1 (Standard Card & Panel):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting over `#F8FAFC`, framed by an ultra-crisp border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Casts an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.02)`.
3. **Surface Layer 2 (Active Telemetry & Floating Maps Controls):** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle satellite-tinted border (`1px solid #BAE6FD`). Casts a crisp elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.05)`.
4. **Surface Layer 3 (Modals, Season Forecast Overlays, Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.04)`.
5. **Dark Mode & Telemetry Overlays:** Dark panels use `#0E1A38` background, framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and subtle back-lighting using `#0284C7` at 5% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft Architectural Shape Language** (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Core Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to telemetry sensor chips, timeline ticks, precision input fields, and tabular data cells. Reflects mathematical rigor and technical calibration.
- **Component Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to standard analytic cards, charts, alerts, and seasonal advisory modules.
- **Container Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved for modal sheets, interactive raster map viewports, and primary floating navigation panels.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Restricted solely to status pills, scientific source tags (e.g., `NASA POWER`), and the bilingual language toggle pill.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** `#0A1128` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#1E293B`. Active click scales to 0.99 with inset micro-contrast. Focus rings use `2px solid #0284C7` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary Sensor Button:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #0284C7` and `#0369A1` text. On hover, fills with `#F0F9FF`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#0A1128` text, hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### Scientific Telemetry Badges (NASA POWER, SMAP, GPM IMERG)
- Composed with strict technical anatomy: a 6px status LED dot, uppercase tracked sensor identifier (`label-sm`), and parameter value.
  - *NASA SMAP (Soil Moisture):* Background `#F0FDF4`, text `#15803D`, border `#BBF7D0`.
  - *GPM IMERG (Precipitation):* Background `#F0F9FF`, text `#0369A1`, border `#BAE6FD`.
  - *NASA POWER (Solar/Temp):* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `#FDE68A`.

### Multi-Season Timeline Nodes & Chips
- Designed for seasonal transitions (e.g., Kharif-1, Kharif-2, Rabi in Bangladesh).
- Connected horizontally with a 2px `#E2E8F0` guideline. Current seasonal node features a dual-ring indicator (`#0284C7` outer pulse ring with `#FFFFFF` inner core) and an active date badge displaying precipitation probability.

### Language Switcher (EN / বাংলা)
- Segmented pill container (`1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#F1F5F9`, height 32px).
- Active segment is an elevated white chip (`#FFFFFF`, shadow level 1) with `#0A1128` semibold text, ensuring legible rendering for both Latin script and Bengali conjunct characters.

### Data Cards & Crop Health Indicators
- Outlined white surfaces (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) with a 3px vertical accent bar on the left edge denoting agronomic risk status (Green = Favorable, Amber = Stress/Caution, Blue = Precipitation Event).
- Metric headers present the localized parameter name, an information tooltip anchor, and bold tabular numbers paired with ISO scientific units (e.g., `m³/m³`, `mm/day`, `MJ/m²/day`).

### Inputs & Selector Fields
- Crisp fields with `1px solid #CBD5E1` borders, `#FFFFFF` fill, `#0A1128` text, and `#64748B` placeholder values. 
- Integrated geospatial location search bars feature satellite coordinate markers and quick-selection chips for regional upazilas and ecological zones.
