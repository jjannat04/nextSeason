Why This Plan

  • check_circle Unlocks 300% cropping intensity: Maximizes annual yield without overdrafting the DTW-04 groundwater command aquifer.
  • check_circle Escapes terminal heat waves: 75-day ultra-short Tori-7 mustard completes pod maturation before late February temperature surges (>32°C).
  • check_circle Fertilizer cost containment: Biological nodulation by BARI Mung-6 deposits 18–22 kg/ha atmospheric nitrogen for the succeeding crop.

Water & Soil Ledger

Hydrological Balance:

Aman harvest preserves sufficient capillary moisture for mustard direct seeding up to Nov 15. Only one single supplemental pump irrigation is needed at 25–28 DAS (flowering).

Soil Pedology & Health:

Rotating Poaceae ➔ Brassicaceae ➔ Fabaceae breaks severe fungal sclerotinia cycles. Green pulse biomass adds ~1.8 t/ha organic matter directly into Rangpur silt loam.
