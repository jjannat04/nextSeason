Module 05

Pareto Non-Dominated Ranking Formulation

Mathematical Definition
Pareto Dominance Definition

A rotation sequence RA is defined to strictly dominate another sequence RB (RA ≻ RB) if and only if:

∀ i ∈ {1, …, M}: fi(RA) ≥ fi(RB)  ∧  ∃ j ∈ {1, …, M}: fj(RA) > fj(RB)
Where objective vector F(R) = [ +NetMargin, −AquiferDrawdown, +SoilSOM, −RiskVaR ]

Why Scalarization Fails: Simple linear equations like J(R) = w1M + w2W + w3S suffer from catastrophic preference distortion. A high net-margin maize monoculture can statistically hide excessive aquifer collapse if given a heavy financial weight.

Our Solution: By maintaining the multi-dimensional Pareto Frontier, our system presents the farmer with 2 to 3 structurally distinct optimal strategies (e.g., Maximum Profit Frontier vs. Aquifer-Conserving Frontier), ensuring full agency and agronomic transparency.

In North-Western Bangladesh (Barind Tract & Tista Floodplain), deep tube-wells (DTW) and shallow tube-wells (STW) draw from the same unconfined Quaternary alluvium aquifer. When neighboring farmers simultaneously pump for flooded Boro rice, their cones of depression intersect, dropping hydraulic head below suction pump limits.

difference Two Distinct Mitigation Levers:

1. Temporal Staggering (Δt/Δp): Staggering transplanting windows by 10–14 days spreads out the peak pumping draw without altering crop variety. Total seasonal volumetric withdrawal remains equal, but peak aquifer depression is smoothed by 28%.

2. Volumetric Crop Substitution (ΔV): Replacing flood-irrigated Boro rice (1,200mm) with Mustard → Mungbean (490mm combined) permanently reduces gross volumetric extraction by 59%, preventing regional water-table collapse.

Module 09

System Boundaries & Known Academic Limitations

Peer Reviewer Transparency
grid_goldenratio 1. Spatial Sub-Pixel Scale

SMAP L4 provides 9km grid resolution. While suitable for regional hydrologic dynamics, micro-topographical depressions (<50m) holding residual puddles require farmer verification through visual moisture confirmation chips in the Planning Tool.

pest_control 2. Biotic & Viral Outbreaks

Radiometers measure physical water, temperature, and radiation. They cannot detect latent soilborne bacterial wilt or airborne insect swarms (e.g. Fall Armyworm). Integrated Pest Management (IPM) guidelines must accompany rotation schedules.

trending_down 3. Macro Market Speculation

Margin calculations are anchored on 5-year median DAM (Department of Agricultural Marketing) farmgate price bands. Sudden macro shocks, trade embargoes, or localized diesel fuel price surges lie outside our biophysical engine.
